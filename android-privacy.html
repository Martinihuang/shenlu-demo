神籙 Android 隱私說明

Updated / 更新：2026-11-25 · Martin Huang · com.martin.shenlu

English

This notice covers Martin Huang’s Android game Shenlu: Mythic Duels (神籙), its testing signup and optional feedback channels. The current test is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Game progress and files

No game account is required. Progress and preferences stay on your device; they are not automatically uploaded or synchronized. Save import and export use Android’s file picker and only the file you choose. Exported backups are under your control. Clearing app data or uninstalling can erase progress; export a backup first.

Google Play installation and updates

Google Play’s update library processes device metadata, app version and installed module/asset-pack information for update checks. Google documents encryption and limited retention. The developer does not receive those raw update records.

Connections and statistics

The Android game does not send the website’s play-start analytics events from its local origin. It may read the public play counter. Network providers may process IP addresses and request headers when serving these connections. The public website has separate visit/play statistics described in its own privacy notice. No advertising SDK, advertising profiling or in-game advertising is included in this build.

Testing signup

Google Groups and Google Play manage test eligibility. Join with the same Google account you use for Play, then opt in on the Play testing page when the test is available. Joining the group alone does not install the game. The group owner can see your membership and email; the group is configured so ordinary members and the public cannot view the member list or member email addresses. You can leave the group and opt out of Play testing separately.

Optional questions and feedback

Threads comments and attachments may be public and require a Threads account. Google Forms is a private backup channel: it does not require sign-in or automatically collect email. Submitted text and optional device/stage details go to the developer to investigate problems and improve the game. Email messages include your address and what you send. Avoid passwords, identity documents or complete saves. We keep feedback only as needed to investigate and respond, then delete it or remove identifying details. Third-party services apply their own retention and privacy practices.

Your choices and contact

You can play without sending feedback. Manage local saves on your device, your own Threads comments on Threads, and Google/Play membership in those services. To request access, correction or deletion of feedback held by the developer, email martinihuang@gmail.com with the approximate date and enough message text to locate it. Do not send a password or identity document. The developer cannot directly erase Google’s or Meta’s independent service records.

This notice will be updated if the game’s data practices change. Public website privacy notice.

繁體中文

本說明適用 Martin Huang 提供的《神籙》Android 遊戲（Shenlu: Mythic Duels）、測試登記與選用回饋管道。本次測試的預定對象為 18 歲以上成人。

遊戲進度與檔案

遊戲不要求註冊帳號。進度與偏好設定留在裝置上，不會自動上傳或跨裝置同步。存檔匯入／匯出使用 Android 檔案選擇器，只處理你選擇的檔案；匯出備份由你自行保管。清除應用程式資料或解除安裝可能使進度遺失，請先匯出備份。

Google Play 安裝與更新

Google Play 更新程式庫會處理裝置中繼資料、應用程式版本及已安裝模組／資產包資訊，用於檢查更新。Google 說明資料會加密並限期保留；開發者不會收到這些原始更新紀錄。

服務連線與統計

Android 遊戲的本機來源不會送出官網的遊玩開始統計事件，但可能讀取公開遊玩計數。服務商提供連線時，仍可能處理 IP 位址及請求標頭。公開官網另有造訪／遊玩統計，詳見官網隱私說明。本封包沒有廣告 SDK、廣告側寫或遊戲內廣告。

測試登記

測試資格由 Google 群組與 Google Play 管理。請用與 Play 相同的 Google 帳號加入群組，待測試開放後另至 Play 頁面加入測試；加入群組不等於已安裝遊戲。群組版主可以查看你的成員資格與 Email；設定限制一般成員及公開訪客查看成員名單與成員 Email。退出群組與退出 Play 測試是分開的操作。

選用的提問與回饋

Threads 留言與附件可能公開，回覆需要 Threads 帳號。Google 表單是私人備用管道，不要求登入，也不自動收集 Email。你提交的文字及選填裝置／關卡資料會交給開發者，用於重現問題與改善遊戲；寄信則會包含寄件地址與你提供的內容。請勿傳送密碼、證件或完整存檔。回饋僅在調查與回覆所需期間保留，之後刪除或移除可識別資訊；第三方服務另依各自政策處理保留期間與隱私。

你的選擇與聯絡方式

你可以不提供回饋而直接遊玩。在裝置上管理存檔、在 Threads 管理自己的留言，並於 Google／Play 分別管理測試資格。若需查詢、更正或刪除開發者持有的回饋，請寄至 martinihuang@gmail.com，提供大概提交時間與足以查找的訊息文字，不需提供密碼或證件。開發者無法直接刪除 Google 或 Meta 獨立持有的服務紀錄。

資料處理方式變更時會更新本說明。官網隱私說明。
